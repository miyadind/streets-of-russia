# Streets of Russia Roadmap

## Current Target

Complete playable campaign v1.0.

## Campaign Structure

The campaign contains seven regions in this order:

1. Far East
2. Siberia
3. Ural
4. Northwest / Saint Petersburg
5. Volga Region
6. South / Sochi
7. Moscow Region

Each region must contain three gameplay screens:

- Screen 1: regular street encounter
- Screen 2: regular street encounter
- Screen 3: regional finale with a small final battle

The third screen of the Moscow Region takes place in the Kremlin. It contains
the final battle of the full campaign. Completing it ends the game and opens
the final ending screen.
